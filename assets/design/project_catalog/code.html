The Monolith Pavilion

Architecture 2024 schedule 450h

A conceptual exhibition space designed to challenge spatial perception through raw concrete forms and strategic natural light apertures. The objective was to create an environment where the architecture itself curates the visitor's pace and focus, forcing contemplation over rapid consumption.
